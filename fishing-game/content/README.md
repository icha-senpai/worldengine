# Versioned content

`species.json` is the authoritative active catalog of all 251 supplied sprite names, preserving numeric species IDs and spelling. Every entry includes allowed ranks and rank-specific rewards/tickets, one home biome, encounter tickets, prototype measurements/value, discovery XP, and completion eligibility. Nidalees Lost Sock is the one fake fish; Sun Fish and Sunfish remain distinct.

`world.json` defines seven biomes and rod families, category splits and level cap 120. The starter Twig is free; later rods and sequential licences are bought from the Trader. Power grants a chance of one additional independent pull. Luck shifts physical size bands. Bait and permanent rod quality are active.

`game-rules.json` defines rules version 7 and the global 60-second accepted-cast cooldown. All equipment and travel changes preserve that cooldown. Rejected operations and receipt replays do not restart it. `crafting.json` defines eight 10-use baits, including green, yellow and pink XP slime; see [XP progression](../docs/xp-progression.md).

Each biome has exactly 1,000,000 conditional fish tickets. Sample the category, species, and weighted physical size band; generate integer measurements and classify their rank. `size-rules.json` publishes the minimum length and weight relative to species typical size. Both must meet the threshold; the highest qualifying rank wins. F means below D in at least one measurement. All 249 ordinary fish allow all ten ranks; Fihs only UUR and the Sock only F. There are 2,492 valid physical bands. Band weights preserve the approved catch probabilities; rank is derived after measurement rather than independently rolled. The measured rank is saved on specimens, receipts, journal entries, and record winners. Species progress keeps ten rank counts while species discovery counts only once. `rarities.json` is the historical reference tier table, not an extra multiplier on the active species weights. `meadow-pond.json` preserves the original eight-fish reference and is not consumed by the module.

Fihs is UUR only and second-rarest. It has 128 tickets in Abyssal Shelf, half any ordinary Abyssal UUR entry's 256. Nidalees Lost Sock stays F rank and has 64 tickets, half Fihs. This gives 0.009984% and 0.004992% per accepted Abyssal cast respectively, including junk and treasure. Both are excluded from the 249-entry ordinary book and from progression requirements. Validation compares exact integer probabilities for every species/rank pair across every biome to preserve the global hierarchy.

Rebuild prototype assignments with `node scripts/build-world-content.mjs`; run `npm run content:validate`, then `node scripts/world-balance.mjs`. Preserve IDs, names, keys, and original image bytes. [Balance report](../docs/world-balance.md) documents probabilities, expected and unlucky waits, content counts, and maintenance. Measurement and economy values are gameplay prototypes pending real-player tuning.
